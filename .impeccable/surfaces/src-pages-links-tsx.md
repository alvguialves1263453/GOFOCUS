OWN-WORLD: Booth Gray #A7AAAF field with Booth Ink #141414 controls; Booth Interior #0B0B0B field with photo-led cards; Big Shoulders signage, Archivo text; Banlek keeps its own blue chip.

STORY: a visitor from Instagram sees who we are, finds their event photos in one tap, then watches reels and browses albums.
