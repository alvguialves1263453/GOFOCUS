# Home (src/pages/Index.tsx)

Scope: the public home page. Mode: Persuade (a prospective client decides whether to trust GoFocus with an event and gets in touch); the albums carry the proof.

Audience and job: people planning a birthday, recital, team event or brand shoot, mostly arriving from Instagram on a phone; they need to see real, finished work in their category within seconds, then open an album or message on Instagram.

Constraints: must scroll and animate smoothly in Safari on a Mac and on phones; no backdrop blur over moving content, no animated CSS filters, no heavy 3D, no full-screen blend layers. Real albums only; no invented claims.

Run note: the owner asked for no questions; the direction below is the assigned roll, taken unattended. Challengers dealt (kraftwerk-man-machine, posada broadside, phosphor terminal, cracktro scroller, forging scale shower, curved-crease shell) were all declined: none identifies the audience or clarifies the product better.

## Direction contract

THESIS: GoFocus is a party photo booth. The home is the booth itself: press the button, the flash fires, and a printed strip of four real photos from one album drops out of the slot. It refuses the category default of a dark full-bleed hero over a grid of cards.

OWN-WORLD: booth-enamel gold (#E2A820) owns the exterior surfaces; the booth interior and print slot are true black; strips are bright photo-paper white with a printed caption line; chrome hardware grey for buttons and plaques. Condensed marquee signage type (Big Shoulders Display) for every headline, Archivo for reading text. Raise, from kraftwerk-man-machine: one colour carries every accent; nothing but the photos is colourful. Raise, from the Posada broadside: signage set large and dense, edge to edge, never timid.

STORY: the visitor understands GoFocus photographs parties and events, sees that every strip is a real album, believes the range from strip after strip, and opens an album or messages on Instagram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the booth front fills the screen. Left (top on phones): the marquee sign "GoFocus" and "Fotografia que transcende", then the round chrome "Tirar uma tira" button with "Falar no Instagram" beside it. Right (below on phones): the black print slot; on load a first strip has already dropped, four photos of one album with its name and photo count printed at the foot; pressing the button counts 3-2-1, flashes white, and prints a strip from another album.

FORM: photo-booth strip, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed:b4355d2d073e31ec.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
